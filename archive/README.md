# Veyra Archive

This directory keeps historical or intermediate material out of the active
repository surface without deleting it. Nothing here is a current product,
architecture, design, implementation, or delivery source of truth.

Do not read archived material during a normal session unless the user names it
or a canonical document explicitly links to it.

## Contents

- `context/` — dated client context and superseded discussion summaries.
- `handoffs/` — stale continuation briefs retained for provenance.
- `design-assets/` — historical standalone and desktop QA screenshots not used
  by the app at runtime.
- `research/raw/` — intermediate competitor profiles and buyer-needs research;
  the active synthesis remains in `outputs/veyra-product-review/` until the
  positioning brainstorm is complete.
- `tooling/manus/` — the Manus workflow queue receipt and invocation metadata;
  it is not an authoritative credit or billing record.

## Archive policy

Archive material when it is useful for provenance but no longer drives current
work. Generated artifacts that are safe and cheap to recreate may instead be
ignored or removed after confirming that no documented workflow depends on
them. Never move active source, current requirements, current tasks, Reticle
state, or application test fixtures here merely to make the root look smaller.
